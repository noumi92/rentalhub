RentalHub — Terms of Service

Effective Date: November 28, 2026 · Pakistan

1. Platform Role

RentalHub connects customers seeking vehicle rentals with independent verified car rental showrooms in Pakistan. Each rental agreement is a direct contract between the customer (hirer) and the showroom owner.

2. Eligibility & Verification

  • Users must be at least 18 years old and provide accurate CNIC, phone, and (for self-drive rentals) valid driving licence information.
  • Showroom owners must provide accurate business, identity, and vehicle registration details.

3. Bookings, Handover & Return

  • Vehicle availability, pricing, advance payments, and security deposits are displayed prior to confirmation.
  • Both parties must complete the handover inspection (odometer, fuel level, photos, and digital agreement) before releasing the vehicle.
  • Traffic challans, e-tickets, fuel consumed, late return charges, and verified return damages are settled in accordance with the signed rental agreement.

4. Fraud Prevention & Disputes

Submission of forged documents, vehicle theft, or willful default may result in account suspension, inclusion in the RentalHub showroom blacklist after review, and legal action under Pakistani law. Users may raise disputes or submit appeals via in-app support or support@rentalhub.pk.
