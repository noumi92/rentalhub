RentalHub — Account & Data Deletion

Option 1: Delete Directly Inside the App (Immediate)

Open the RentalHub Android app, sign in, and go to Settings → Delete Account. Confirm the prompt to permanently delete your account immediately.

Option 2: Request Deletion via Web

If you no longer have the app installed, submit your registered account details below. Requests are verified and processed within 7 business days.

What Data Is Deleted vs. Retained

  • Permanently Deleted: Login credentials, user profile, CNIC and driving licence KYC documents, saved search locations, in-app notifications, and push notification tokens.
  • Retained Where Required by Law: Completed rental booking receipts, financial settlement records, and active dispute/fraud records may be retained in restricted/anonymized form for up to 5 years for legal, tax, and dispute compliance.
