RentalHub — Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 28, 2026 · Pakistan

1. Who We Are

RentalHub (pk.rentalhub.rentalhub) is a car rental marketplace and showroom fleet management platform operating in Pakistan. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your personal information when you use our Android app or web services.

2. Information We Collect

  • Account & Profile Details: Name, phone number, email address, city, and residential/showroom address.
  • Government Identity & KYC Verification: CNIC number, CNIC front/back images, driving licence number and photos, customer/owner/guarantor digital signatures, and rear-camera 4-finger verification photographs (collected solely as visual verification photos — no biometric fingerprint scanner SDK is used).
  • Vehicle & Rental Records: Booking dates, vehicle photos, handover/return odometer and fuel readings, signed rental agreements, and payment/deposit receipts.
  • Location Data: Approximate and precise foreground location (only with your permission) to discover nearby showrooms and set showroom map pins. We do not collect background location.
  • Camera & Photos: Accessed only when you actively capture KYC documents, vehicle photos, or scan booking QR codes.
  • Device & App Activity: Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) push tokens, app diagnostics, and booking/notification history.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • Verify customer, guarantor, and showroom identities to prevent vehicle theft and fraud.
  • Process car rental bookings, generate digital rental agreements, and record handover/return settlements.
  • Send transactional booking updates and security notifications via push notifications.
  • Operate the Shared Showroom Blacklist & Dispute Tribunal: confirmed fraud, theft, or severe default cases may be shared with verified partner showrooms on RentalHub to protect fleet owners. Affected users may submit an appeal through RentalHub support.

4. Data Security & Third-Party Services

All data is encrypted in transit using HTTPS/TLS. We use Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Cloud Functions, Cloud Messaging) and secure HTTPS object storage to host application data and documents. We do not sell your personal information to third parties or advertisers.

5. Data Retention & Account Deletion

You may permanently delete your account at any time inside the app under Settings → Delete Account, or submit a web deletion request at Account & Data Deletion.

Upon deletion, your login credentials, profile, CNIC/licence KYC documents, notifications, and push tokens are permanently removed. Completed rental receipts, financial settlement records, and active fraud/dispute records may be retained in restricted or anonymized form for up to 5 years where required by Pakistani law.

6. Age Requirement (18+)

RentalHub is intended strictly for adults aged 18 and older who are legally eligible to rent or operate motor vehicles in Pakistan. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 18.

7. Contact Us

For privacy questions, blacklist appeals, or data requests, contact us at support@rentalhub.pk. Also see our Terms of Service.
